The five filters (sequential, per the book):

F1 · Durable Competitive Advantage. Qualitative in the book (Porter's five forces, moats — Western Union, Visa, Fastenal, Waters). Not computable from price data. This tool proxies it with the book's own operational hook: a company with a real moat sustains ROIC > COC across a full cycle. Names passing the multi-year ROIC history get a green F1; all survivors still carry an F1 MANUAL reminder to eyeball the moat yourself. The book is explicit that this filter eliminates most of the ~2,800 NYSE names before the quantitative work.

F2 · Free Cash Flow Yield (margin of safety). FCF / Enterprise Value, where FCF = Operating Cash Flow − CapEx and EV = Market Cap + Debt − Cash. Must clear a multiple over the 10-yr Treasury (the exact multiple is proprietary; proxy = 2×). The book's Amazon example (0.224% FCF yield vs 2.75% risk-free) is the anti-pattern.

F3 · Return on Invested Capital. ROIC = NOPAT / Invested Capital, NOPAT = Operating Income × (1 − tax rate), Invested Capital = Total Assets − Excess Cash − Non-interest-bearing current liabilities. Must exceed COC now and show a knack for it over time (book: 10yr; proxy: as many years as Polygon returns, default ≥3). Morningstar's ~15% wide-moat bar is the current-year floor.

F4 · Long-Term Debt / Free Cash Flow. ≤ 3 years (author is deliberately stricter than Buffett's 5). Snapshot of the latest filing, not a trend. Cummins 1.24yr vs Caterpillar 5.29yr is the worked example.

F5 · The 52-Week Low. Trading at/near its 52-week low (examples entered 3–10% above). Optional TTM-return gate encodes Ch.12's "embrace a −25% trailing return." This is the last filter — value = where a structurally sound company meets depressed sentiment.

Data caveats (baked into the UI, per your standing preference):

• CapEx is not always a clean line in Polygon's standardized financials. The tool tries several field aliases and, if capex is truly absent, falls back to FCF ≈ operating cash flow and flags it approx in the log — treat those FCF-yield / LTD-FCF numbers as optimistic.
• Tax rate for NOPAT is derived from income_tax_expense / pre-tax income when available, else assumes 21%.
• 10-year history is impossible on the ~5-yr Polygon plan; F1/F3-history are proxied and flagged.
• ETFs (SPY/GLD/TLT) have no financials — they're scored on F5 only and marked N/A elsewhere, since the book's fundamental filters don't apply to funds.

Score = weighted blend of margin-of-safety depth (FCF ×RF), ROIC cushion over COC, debt headroom, and proximity to the low — a ranking aid only. The book's actual output is the top 25 names that pass all filters, rebalanced every 6–12 months.

Single-file client-side tool. No backend; your key never leaves the browser except in direct calls to api.polygon.io. This screens for candidates — it is not investment advice, and F1 always requires your own moat judgment. Rebalance discipline (every 6/12 months, sell on filter failure) is the strategy, not the screen.
