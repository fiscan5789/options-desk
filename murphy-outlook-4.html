How to read this. Scores are Murphy's indicator conditions computed from daily price/volume over each horizon's lookback — not a price forecast. OI walls are strikes with the largest open interest: heavy call OI above spot tends to act as resistance / a magnet, heavy put OI below spot as support. Walls are a positioning read, not a guarantee — they can break or shift as dealers hedge. Each tenor panel anchors to the single expiration carrying the most total open interest within a window around its target (30/60/90/120 DTE), so it snaps to the dominant monthly/quarterly OPEX date rather than the literal nearest expiration. Technical analysis is probabilistic. Not financial advice — every read is a hypothesis to confirm.
