How this reads volume · scan log
This follows Anna Coulling's three steps: micro (each candle against its volume), macro (the last few candles), and global (congestion, breakouts and the 15- and 30-minute charts). Coulling judges each volume bar as low, average, high or ultra high against recent bars. Intraday ETF volume follows a strong time-of-day pattern (heavy at the open and close, light at midday), so each 5-minute bar is compared with the median volume for that same 5-minute slot over the prior 20 sessions. Low is under 0.7× normal, average 0.7–1.3×, high 1.3–2×, very high 2–3×, ultra high 3× or more. Spread (bar range) is judged the same way: narrow under 0.7×, wide 1.3× or more. Effort vs. result (Wyckoff's third law): a wide candle should carry high volume, and a narrow candle low volume; the mismatches are the anomalies. Signals read: shooting star, hammer, hanging man, long-legged doji (low volume = trap), wide and narrow candles with volume anomalies, stopping volume, topping out volume, selling climax at the top and buying climax at the bottom (Coulling's naming), and low-volume tests of supply and demand after congestion. Congestion is defined by Coulling's isolated pivots (the middle candle of three makes a higher high and higher low, or a lower low and lower high). A breakout needs a clear close outside the zone; it is treated as validated at 1.3× normal volume or more and as a possible fake-out under 0.9×. Every signal is checked against the bars that follow (confirmed, failed or awaiting), in line with her patience principle. Volume at price is approximated by spreading each 5-minute bar's volume evenly across its range; true tick-level profiles will differ. The engine history replays this same engine every 30 minutes on every loaded session using only bars up to that time. It checks whether the close went the outlook's way and whether price moved 0.2 ADR in that direction before 0.2 ADR against. Coulling herself warns that VPA is an art and software cannot do it for you. Treat this tool as a structured second opinion, not a replacement for reading the chart.
